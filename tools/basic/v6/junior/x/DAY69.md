|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| recording | record (기록하다) · recorder (녹음기) | — | a sound or song that is kept on a machine | Our teacher played three short recordings of bird songs in science class. | 선생님은 과학 시간에 새소리 녹음 세 개를 짧게 틀어 주셨어요. |
| saving | save (저축하다) · saver (저축하는 사람) | — | keeping money to use later instead of spending it | My savings from three months were enough to buy a new backpack. | 석 달 동안 모은 내 저축으로 새 가방을 사기에 충분했어요. |
| technique | technical (기술적인) · technician (기술자) | — | a special way to do a skill well | Our cooking teacher showed us three easy techniques for cutting vegetables. | 요리 선생님은 채소를 써는 쉬운 기술 세 가지를 보여 주셨어요. |
| sandy | sand (모래) | — | full of tiny pieces of rock, like on a beach | The north side of the island is sandier than the south side. | 섬의 북쪽은 남쪽보다 모래가 더 많아요. |
| effective | effect (효과) · effectively (효과적으로) | — | working well and giving the result you want | Yuna thinks drawing pictures is the most effective way to remember new words. | 유나는 그림을 그리는 것이 새 단어를 기억하는 가장 효과적인 방법이라고 생각해요. |
| formal | formality (격식) · formally (격식 있게) | — | following the polite rules used at serious events | Our principal's welcome speech was more formal than the class president's speech. | 교장 선생님의 환영 연설은 반장의 연설보다 더 격식 있었어요. |
| useless | use (사용하다) · useful (유용한) | — | not helpful at all because it cannot do its job | Without batteries, the radio was more useless than an empty box. | 건전지가 없으니 라디오는 빈 상자보다도 더 쓸모없었어요. |
| admire | admiration (감탄) · admirable (훌륭한) | — | to look at someone or something with great respect | Jiho stood still, admiring the big rainbow over the playground. | 지호는 가만히 서서 운동장 위의 커다란 무지개를 감탄하며 바라보았어요. |
| consist | consistent (한결같은) | — | to be made up of several parts | Mina drew a picture consisting of many small colorful circles. | 미나는 작고 알록달록한 동그라미 여러 개로 이루어진 그림을 그렸어요. |
| associate | association (연관) | — | to connect two things together in your mind | Sweet smells are often associated with spring flowers and warm weather. | 달콤한 향기는 흔히 봄꽃과 따뜻한 날씨와 연결돼요. |
| proper | properly (알맞게) · improper (부적절한) | proper noun (고유명사) | right and correct for a certain place or time | A quiet voice is more proper than a loud voice in a museum. | 박물관에서는 큰 목소리보다 조용한 목소리가 더 알맞아요. |
| wrap | wrapper (포장지) | — | to cover something all around with paper or cloth | Grandma wrapped the warm bread in a clean cloth for our trip. | 할머니는 우리 여행을 위해 따뜻한 빵을 깨끗한 천에 싸 주셨어요. |
| shoot | — | shoot hoops (농구 슛 연습을 하다) | to take a picture or send a ball toward a goal | Tom shot the ball from far away, and the whole team cheered. | 톰이 멀리서 공을 던져 넣자 팀 전체가 환호했어요. |
| injury | injure (다치게 하다) · injured (다친) | — | harm to the body from a fall or an accident | Wearing the right shoes can prevent many injuries during PE class. | 알맞은 신발을 신으면 체육 시간에 많은 부상을 막을 수 있어요. |
| Halloween | — | — | a fall holiday when kids wear funny costumes | — | — |
| charming | charm (매력) · charmingly (매력적으로) | — | very pleasant and attractive in a way people like | The old bridge looked even more charming under the morning sun than at night. | 그 오래된 다리는 밤보다 아침 햇살 아래에서 훨씬 더 매력적으로 보였어요. |
| defeat | undefeated (무패의) | admit defeat (패배를 인정하다) | the result when you lose a game or contest | Even after two defeats, the team kept practicing hard every day. | 두 번 졌는데도 그 팀은 매일 열심히 계속 연습했어요. |
| thigh | — | — | the top part of your leg above the knee | Soccer players build strong thighs by kicking and running every day. | 축구 선수들은 매일 차고 달리면서 튼튼한 허벅지를 만들어요. |
| bury | — | — | to put something in the ground and cover it with soil | The squirrel buried acorns under the big oak tree before winter. | 다람쥐는 겨울이 오기 전에 큰 참나무 밑에 도토리를 묻어 두었어요. |
| command | commander (지휘관) | have a good command of (…을 능숙하게 다루다) | to tell someone strongly what they must do | The queen in the fairy tale commanded the baker to make a giant cake. | 동화 속 여왕은 제빵사에게 거대한 케이크를 만들라고 명령했어요. |
| researcher | research (연구, 조사) | — | a person who studies a subject carefully to learn new facts | Researchers at the zoo counted the penguins every morning for a month. | 동물원의 연구원들은 한 달 동안 매일 아침 펭귄 수를 세었어요. |
| starve | starvation (굶주림) | — | to suffer because you do not have enough food | After the long hike, we were starving, so we ate lunch quickly. | 긴 하이킹 뒤에 우리는 몹시 배가 고파서 점심을 빨리 먹었어요. |
| rewarm | warm (따뜻한) · warmth (따뜻함) | — | to make cold food hot once more | Rewarming leftover pizza in the oven takes about ten minutes. | 남은 피자를 오븐에 다시 데우는 데는 약 10분이 걸려요. |
| championship | champion (우승자) | — | a big contest to find the best team or player | Our school's swim team has won two championships in the past five years. | 우리 학교 수영 팀은 지난 5년 동안 선수권 대회에서 두 번 우승했어요. |
| arrest | — | under arrest (체포된) | to catch someone and take him or her to the police | In the picture book, the police officer arrested a fox who stole the pies. | 그림책에서 경찰관은 파이를 훔친 여우를 체포했어요. |
| moral | morality (도덕) · morally (도덕적으로) | — | following ideas about what is right and wrong | Many stories for children end with morals about honesty and kindness. | 어린이를 위한 많은 이야기는 정직과 친절에 관한 교훈으로 끝나요. |
| insist | insistence (고집) · insistent (고집하는) | — | to say strongly that something must be done | Dad kept insisting that we take an umbrella because clouds were coming. | 구름이 몰려오고 있다며 아빠는 우산을 가져가라고 계속 고집하셨어요. |
| unless | — | — | a word that means if something does not happen | — | — |
| declare | declaration (선언) | — | to say something in an official way to everyone | The sign on the door declares that the shop opens at nine. | 문에 붙은 표지판은 가게가 아홉 시에 문을 연다고 알려요. |
| behave | behavior (행동) | — | to act in a certain way, good or bad | The twins were behaving like little tigers at the shopping mall. | 쌍둥이는 쇼핑몰에서 아기 호랑이들처럼 행동하고 있었어요. |
